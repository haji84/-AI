# Formal violations and corrective actions

Canonical specification: docs/SPECIFICATION.md§12–13,17,39,47. Base: main386582ce, tree10add6f0, CI37511281181Green. Continuous completion authorization includes implementation/testing/PR/merge; ordinary design reapproval is not requested.

Preserve InspectionFinding as an inspection observation. New ViolationCase records an alleged issue, missing information and confirmation steps independently. Optional finding links must belong to the same facility. AI provenance remains candidate-only, with no direct update to findings or official records. Candidate creation does not assert a violation.

Human review requires original evidence, formal-procedure originals, and at least one Human-approved effective LegalRuleVersion with structured LegalRuleCitation. Resolve the official enabled LegalSource, source-document version/hash/URL, provision key/text/hash and cited snapshot. Bind all this with facility/finding versions and original SHA256 in a review snapshot. Confirm requires separate permission, expected version, affirmative Human acknowledgment and reason, and an unchanged snapshot. Reject missing/unapproved/changed/unreadable sources. No legal conditions or institutional procedure is invented; Human supplies and verifies the applicable Rule and actual procedure originals.

Confirmed case factual content is immutable. Changes use a linked new candidate revision; withdrawal retains decision/history. No destructive deletes. A record's formal provenance remains viewable even if later rules change; new confirmations cannot use changed review evidence.

Measures (guidance/order/disposition) attach to a case. Orders/dispositions require a confirmed violation; Human advisory guidance may precede formal violation confirmation and never changes the case into a formal violation. Measures contain Human text, originals, deadline and explicit procedure evidence. A measure starts draft and requires separate review and formal confirmation; order/disposition cannot bypass procedure proof. No automatic notice numbers. Correction tasks track instruction, deadline, response, verification and completion with immutable append-only events. Response and completion require originals; Human verification requires an explicit result/reason; completion requires positive verification and evidence. Case completion requires all noncancelled correction tasks completed and at least one task; advisory completion uses resolved_candidate with no confirmed_by; it records a Human action, not a claim that a machine assessed compliance.

Use canonical transaction account lock/session revalidation, row-version CAS, same per-department DB/runtime/original boundaries, and existing audit. Distinct read/create/update/review/approve permissions plus source facility/inspection/document/legal RBAC. List/filter/offset/limit, due dates, source links, candidate/official badges, and all Human transitions are browser accessible. Search is permission-aware. No client installation or Excel runtime dependency.

Tables: violation_cases, violation_measures, corrective_actions, correction_events; Migration049 additive. Export includes source snapshots and Human status. PostgreSQL races, original tamper/stale citation/missing source/read revocation and actual Chromium workflow are mandatory gates. Real institutional procedure/law adoption remains Human acceptance; synthetic tests never assert a real law or formal decision.

Instruction/deadline changes invalidate prior verification. Verification binds the latest response and its original hashes; completion rechecks the response/verification chain and adds completion proof. Open correction tasks and confirmed orders must be explicitly resolved before withdrawal/replacement; there is no automatic drop of obligations.
