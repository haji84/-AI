## Selected design

Use a dedicated hazardous evaluation model/service/router under the existing hazardous module permissions and source-closure guards. Add a hazardous domain to the existing Human rule-authoring/version/citation machinery with domain-specific closed condition/outcome validation. Additive SQL056 is provisional until055 is canonical; historical SQL and reserved051 remain unchanged.

The evaluator is deterministic. It accepts a bounded installation version and explicit evaluation date, freezes exact installation/material facts and the selected approved/effective rule versions, and emits candidates only. It never creates a formal violation, permit decision, or approval. An independent Human review requires the current candidate version, explicit acknowledgement, reason and unchanged installation/rule/source snapshots. No formal rule or threshold is seeded without accountable Human approval of official primary sources.

Material names and installation category labels are literal registered facts, not inferred legal classifications. Evaluate each material independently; retain its source row index/name/quantity/unit. Compare numeric quantities using bounded finite Decimal values, preserving original text, and require exact explicit unit compatibility. Do not silently convert units, combine unlike quantities, infer a material class, or report compliance from absent data. Unsupported/missing facts produce an explicit unresolved result; no approved applicable rule means coverage unavailable, not compliant.

Rules retain their original legal source document version, hash, provision citations, effective interval, version, status and condition/outcome snapshot. Before evaluating, reading, counting or reviewing, recheck hazardous/facility/legal/original-document source permissions; source hashes must be server-owned. Structured primary-source citations are mandatory for this new domain even where older domains allow legacy references. An inaccessible source causes fail-closed response, not a partial source-less legal conclusion.
