## Delivered behavior

- Separate OperationalAsset SKU registry with immutable asset_id, operational durable/consumable/drug classification, configured unit, exact reorder threshold, optimistic edits, authorized zero-stock/zero-outstanding Human retirement and audit. Installed FacilityEquipment is unchanged.
- Physical AssetLocation entities reuse common building_id and Task2 vehicle_id. They can be edited/paused with version checks; locations containing stock or outstanding loans cannot be paused.
- AssetLot records preserve batch code, provenance, optional common Document and independently approved expiry dates beneath a single SKU. Expiry entry requires explicit asset.approve permission and records the Human approver/time. Multiple expiry batches do not clone the drug/SKU master.
- Versioned lot/location balances; receive, issue, transfer, loan and return are transactional. Asset and balance rows are locked, CAS checks enforce expected_version, transfers conserve stock, insufficient/negative/overflow stock is rejected and ledger/audit writes commit together. Request-bound unique idempotency keys return the original movement on an exact retry and reject changed reuse.
- Loans reuse active common Employee IDs, preserve batch/location/quantity and outstanding quantity. Returns cannot exceed outstanding stock or precede the recorded loan date. Inactive/missing Employees are rejected on new handling/borrowing. Borrower/handler details and loan free text are excluded from unauthorized history/export; dedicated borrower.read/manage permissions protect detail and actions.
- Inspection, repair, renewal, pressure test, calibration, disposal and expiry write-off use draft → Human review → Human approval. Official dates and stock change only on approval. Review snapshots bind asset/lot versions and linked service/lot Document SHA256 under locks. Changed proof or source versions reject approval409. Approved service records and movement history have no edit/delete route; cancellation is restricted to unapproved service drafts/reviews.
- Pressure-test, use, calibration, service, lot-expiry and loan-return warnings; reorder quantities are candidates and never place purchases. Expired lots cannot be ordinarily issued/loaned. Receiving/returning/relocating expired stock preserves physical truth; removal requires a Human-approved expiry/disposal service.
- Business dates use server-owned FIRE_AI_ASSET_BUSINESS_TIMEZONE (default Asia/Tokyo). /assets/policy exposes the declared timezone/current business date. A caller-selected report as_of cannot alter stock expiry enforcement. Expiry dates are inclusive through their business date; midnight UTC/JST regression coverage verifies the boundary.
- Movement occurred_on is distinct from immutable created_at capture time. Historical imports retain their source business date and never infer dates from filenames. Loan loaned_on/due and return chronology are validated. Future completed movements/services are rejected.
- Explicit assets-v1 CSV/XLSX schemas, actual content sniffing/validation, formula rejection, size/row limits, transactional dry-run, bound preview hash/version/owner/expiry and explicit all-row confirm. Confirmation rechecks the same direct-action and source permissions and rolls back every row on conflict. Common source Document is optional for manual ledgers; upload SHA, preview and applied IDs preserve provenance when supplied independently. Exports expose authorized source Document IDs and import-preview/hash lineage, neutralize formulas with an explicit formula-prefix-v1 text_encoding marker (preserving literal source apostrophes on replay), preserve exact decimal/unit/date values, and use the same query service as API lists. Official/reviewed signatures/state cannot be imported.
- Existing /ui/ shell header/session/API/escape/modal conventions are reused. PC forms cover registry/place/lot registration, stock moves, loan/return, service drafts/review/approval/cancel, retirement, warnings, reorder candidates and bulk imports/exports. Batch/provenance filtering and paginated collection make lots beyond the first 200 accessible; lot search hits navigate to their parent registry. Zero values are preserved. Frontend API arithmetic sends quantity/money strings.
- Additive main/router, RBAC roles/permissions, module operational_assets manifest and unified search hooks. No tenant header/body routing, external paid dependency or AI availability dependency was introduced.
